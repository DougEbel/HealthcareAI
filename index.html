Future Healthcare Information Architecture

Healthcare is generating more information than ever before, yet physicians often lack the time and tools to assemble the small portion of a patient's history that matters for today's clinical decision. This series explores an information architecture intended to address that challenge while preserving the strengths of today's healthcare information systems.

This series explores how a national healthcare information architecture could complement existing healthcare information systems by creating a trustworthy longitudinal patient history that enables better clinical decisions, reduces administrative cost, and provides a stronger foundation for AI-assisted healthcare.
